Theme: **bounce back**

Idea: Conquer a 2d 10x10 grid, where players and enemies are placed at random outside a 3x3 of them. 

each round the player gets two points. each point can be used towards gaining tiles.

the player can launch through their own tiles (for 1 point) and hit enemies to reduce the amount needed to defeat them, but only when they are right next to their tile. 

enemy tiles can be claimed 4 points, and enemies can be defeated for 8 points, and unclaimed tiles are 1 point.
